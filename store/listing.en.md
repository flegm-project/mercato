- Description:

Two clubs. One year. Who is it?

Mercato turns three decades of real football transfers into a guessing
game. Every question shows a real move: the club a player left, the club
he joined, and the year. You name the footballer.

EASY MODE
Four options per question, drawn from the mainstream transfers everyone
argues about. Fast rounds of ten questions.

HARDCORE MODE
No options. Type the name, with three lives per question and free hints
when you need them: nationality, position, then the shape of the surname.
The matching engine tolerates typos and accents, knows nicknames, and
refuses a bare surname when two players share it.

REAL DATA, NOTHING INVENTED
1,900+ real transfers, 500+ players, 400+ clubs, from 1985 to today.
Facts only: no invented moves.

PLAYS ANYWHERE
The whole game ships in the app: no account, no sign-up, fully playable
offline. In English, French and Spanish.

FAIR MONETISATION
Free with ads. One single purchase removes them forever. No coins, no
energy, no subscriptions.
