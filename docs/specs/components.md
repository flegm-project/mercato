# Components

## Button
Ink border 5px, radius 20 to 26, solid shadow 8 to 10px, `translateY(5px)` on press.
- Primary: yellow #FFE24A on ink text.
- Secondary: ivory #FBFAF4.
- Destructive: coral #FF5C63, white text.
- Ghost: transparent, 3px white 24% border.

## Answer button
Idle #0F1A66 white text. Correct #2FE0A0 ink text. Wrong #FF5C63 white text. Unpicked after the round: idle at 40% opacity. Disabled once answered.

## Transfer card
Ink header (kind chip left, year right). Body centred: origin club 19px grey, arrow, destination club 30px ink. Border and solid shadow switch to green-deep or coral-deep with the verdict.

## Progress pips
One pip per question, 9px tall, 3px ink border. Green correct, coral wrong, yellow live, ink 45% pending.

## Lives
Three 13px dots, coral when available, ink 45% when spent. Hardcore only.

## Score pill
Unbounded 900 22px, ivory at rest, green when the last answer was correct, coral when wrong. The bump ("+3" or "0") floats up from under the pill over 1.5s.

## Ball chip
Ink pill, ivory ball with ink outline, yellow number. Balls are the soft currency, earned at the end of a round.

## Toggle
52x30 track, ink border, green when on, ivory knob.

## Tab bar
Ink panel, 3 tabs, active tab is a yellow rounded block. Fixed at the bottom, inside the same 16px gutters as the content.
